01/ About.

I'm William Schiesser III, a graphic designer and multimedia artist based in New Jersey. I make posters, digital art, videos, motion graphics, and pretty much anything I can get creative with.

I like designs that feel personal. A little messy, kind of experimental, and not like something pulled straight from a template.
