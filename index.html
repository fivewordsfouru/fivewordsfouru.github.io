The
Director

I'm William Schiesser — a graphic designer and videographer based in New Jersey. I started making graphic design in the 5th grade and have been creating ever since.

Whether it's underground music videos, album art, commercial design, or motion graphics, I don't rely on a single preset look. Every project dictates its own visual language. I am currently available for freelance, collaborations, and projects that require a unique perspective.
